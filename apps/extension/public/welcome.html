How it works

Four steps. You only do the first one once.

Step 1 of 4

Pin WatchSync

Chrome tucks new extensions behind the puzzle icon in the toolbar. Click it, then the pin next to WatchSync, so it's always one click away.

Step 2 of 4

Create a room, or join one

Click WatchSync and enter your name. Create a room, then send your friends the link or the 6-letter code.

Got a link from a friend? Open it, or type the code in WatchSync.

Step 3 of 4

Open the same title

Each of you opens it on Netflix, Prime Video or JioHotstar, signed in to your own account. If you're on different titles, WatchSync offers to open the right one.

Step 4 of 4

Watch like you always do

Play, pause and skip as usual. Everyone follows.

  • Nobody gets left behind. If someone is buffering or gets an ad, the room waits for them, then carries on together.
  • Start with 3-2-1 counts everyone in: a quick ready check, then the countdown.

What WatchSync can see

WatchSync only sees whether the video is playing, where it is, and its title. It can't see your passwords, payments or other sites.
