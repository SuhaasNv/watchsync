# WatchSync v0.2.0: Chat and reactions

Talk with your room while you watch, without leaving the film. Works with Netflix, Prime Video and JioHotstar in Chrome and Brave.

## What you can do

- **Chat beside the player.** Each message shows the moment in the film it was sent at. Friends who join later can read what was said before.
- **See what got through.** A message says "Sending" until the room has it, and "Not sent" with Retry if it didn't.
- **Never miss a message.** Unread messages show as a count on the on-page pill and on the toolbar icon.
- **React.** Tap a reaction and it floats on everyone's screen with your name.
- **Room activity in one place.** Plays, pauses, jumps, joins and leaves show as lines in the chat. Turn the on-page notices off if you prefer; the chat keeps them.
- **Open chat quickly**, from the popup or with Alt+Shift+W (Control+Shift+W on a Mac).
- **Rooms survive our updates.** When the WatchSync server updates, your room comes back by itself with the same code, people, title and position. The page tells you what is happening while it reconnects.
- **No surprise updates.** A new version of the extension waits until you leave the room.

## Privacy

Chat passes through the room service and is kept in memory only while the room exists (the last 200 messages). It is never logged and is deleted when the room ends or the server restarts. It is encrypted on the way, but not end to end. Reactions are never stored. The chat panel runs inside the extension, so the streaming page can't read what you type.

## Tested

- Automated: lint, types, unit tests, every extension flow end to end against a stand-in player (chat, reactions, room activity, popup, reconnects), the room service's tests, and accessibility (axe, WCAG 2.2 AA).
- Not yet checked with two people on the real streaming services.
