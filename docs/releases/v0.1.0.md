# WatchSync v0.1.0: Streaming Sync

Watch together with friends in other cities, each in your own browser tab and on your own account, in step. Works with Netflix, Prime Video and JioHotstar in Chrome and Brave.

## What you can do

- **Make a room and send the link.** Friends join from the link or the code and are offered the title you're on.
- **Everyone controls.** Play, pause and skips on the service's own player reach everyone, with a note saying who did it.
- **Nobody gets left behind.** When someone's video is loading or on an ad, the room waits, says why and how long, and starts again together.
- **Start together, Pause together, Sync everyone.** A 3-2-1 for the opening, one button to pause for everyone, one to bring everyone to your exact moment.
- **Switch titles freely.** The room follows the last title anyone picks; friends are offered it and choose.
- **Next episode together**, title names everywhere, and a small on-page pill that shows who's here.
- **Recovers.** Wi-Fi drops, closed tabs, browser restarts and updates don't break the room; friends see who left and who's back.

## Tested

- Automated: lint, types, unit tests, every extension flow end to end against a stand-in player, the room service's tests, accessibility (axe, WCAG 2.2 AA), and the website.
- By two people on real accounts: Netflix, and JioHotstar from India with ads. Prime Video's latest fixes wait for the next two-person run.
