# WatchSync v0.1.0: Streaming Sync (release candidate)

Watch together with friends in different places, each in your own Chrome tab and on your own account, perfectly in step. Works with Netflix, Prime Video and JioHotstar.

## What you can do

- **Create a room and share a link.** Friends open it, enter their name, and land on the title you're watching.
- **Everyone controls.** Play, pause and skip on the service's own player; everyone follows within half a second and sees who did it.
- **Nobody gets left behind.** If someone's video is loading or showing an ad, the room waits for them and says why, then everyone resumes together. Nobody else does this.
- **Start together.** A countdown starts every player at the same moment.
- **Stays in sync by itself.** Small drift is fixed quietly; if you fall far behind, one click on Sync brings you back. Watch on your own for a bit if you want.
- **Next episode together**, and a small on-page pill that shows who's here and in sync, also in full screen.
- **Recovers.** Wi-Fi drops, browser restarts and closed tabs don't break the room.

## Before you rely on it

This is a release candidate: everything is tested automatically against a stand-in player (31 browser tests, 37 service tests, accessibility checks). It still needs the two-person test on real Netflix, Prime Video and JioHotstar accounts. See Known issues in `CHANGELOG.md`.
