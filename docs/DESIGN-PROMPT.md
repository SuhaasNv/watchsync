# WATCHSYNC — MASTER UI/UX DESIGN PROMPT

## Create the complete production-quality WatchSync product experience in Fable

You are designing **WatchSync**, a premium macOS-first social watch-party application.

This is NOT a simple landing page design.

This is NOT a collection of disconnected mockups.

This is a **complete product design system, interaction model, screen library, responsive behavior system, and clickable product prototype** covering the entire WatchSync product roadmap from **V0.1 through V1.0**.

Your output should feel like a product that could genuinely be shipped by a world-class startup.

# 2. PRODUCT

Product name:

**WatchSync**

Tagline direction:

**Watch together. Feel together.**

WatchSync allows people to watch content together remotely while communicating through camera, microphone, chat, reactions, and synchronized playback.

The fundamental experience is:

> “I want watching something with my friends remotely to feel almost like we're sitting in the same room.”

The product must make the distance between people feel smaller.

---

# 3. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

The UI should communicate three things immediately:

### 1. Presence

I can see the people I'm watching with.

### 2. Synchronization

We're watching the same moment.

### 3. Simplicity

I don't need to understand networking, WebRTC, rooms, extensions, playback state, or synchronization technology.

The technology should disappear behind the experience.

---

# 4. IMPORTANT PRODUCT BOUNDARIES

Design for legitimate media usage.

WatchSync does NOT:

* bypass DRM
* decrypt protected streaming
* capture protected video
* extract DRM keys
* circumvent HDCP
* proxy protected streaming content
* download protected media

For protected streaming services, users watch their own authorized browser sessions while WatchSync synchronizes playback state.

For local/user-owned/authorized media, WatchSync may support direct media sharing.

The UI must never imply illegal streaming or DRM circumvention.
