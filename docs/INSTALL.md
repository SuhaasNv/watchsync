# Installing WatchSync (v0.1, for friends)

WatchSync keeps your Netflix, Prime Video or JioHotstar tab in sync with your friends' tabs. Each of you uses your own account.

## Install

1. Download the zip from [watchsync.space](https://watchsync.space) (or [directly](https://github.com/SuhaasNv/watchsync/releases/latest/download/watchsync-extension.zip)) and unzip it. Keep the folder; Chrome loads it from there.
2. In Chrome, open `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
5. Pin WatchSync: click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to WatchSync.

Chrome may show a banner about developer-mode extensions when it starts. That is expected for an unpacked extension.

## Watch together

1. Click the WatchSync icon and enter your name.
2. One person clicks **Create a room** and sends the link (or the 6-letter code).
3. Everyone else opens the link, or types the code in the popup and clicks **Join room**.
4. Open the same title on Netflix, Prime Video or JioHotstar. If you're on different titles, WatchSync offers to open the right one.
5. Play, pause and skip as usual: everyone follows. If someone buffers or gets an ad, the room waits for them.

## Updating

Replace the folder with the new version's files, then click the reload arrow on WatchSync in `chrome://extensions`.

## Problems

- **"This room is no longer available"**: the room ended (everyone left for 15 minutes, or the service restarted). Ask for a new link.
- **Reconnecting… for a long time**: check your internet connection; WatchSync keeps retrying.
- **Live streams** can't be synced yet.
